Frequently Asked Questions

Does this work in Google Sheets AND Microsoft Excel?

Yes. All formulas (SUMIFS, INDEX/MATCH, COUNTIF) use universal syntax with zero VBA macros. Open directly in Excel or upload to Google Drive > Open with Google Sheets.

Can I change '$' to Euros (€), Pounds (£) or another currency?

Yes, in 5 seconds. Select the currency columns and use Format > Number > Currency (Google Sheets) or Ctrl+1 (Microsoft Excel).

Are both English and Spanish versions included?

Yes! Every .ZIP download contains standalone English and Spanish workbooks so you can use whichever language you prefer.

How do I clear the sample transactions?

Simply select and delete the sample input rows in the log tabs (leaving formula columns like Auto-Month or Net ($) untouched) and start entering your own numbers.
